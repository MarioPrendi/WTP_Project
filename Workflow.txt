Objective: Develop a functional travel website where visitors can explore destinations, 
create personalized trip plans, save favorite destinations and submit simulated bookings.

Website Structure
Page			    Functionality
Home			    Introduction and featured destinations
Destinations		Browse, search, filter and sort destinations
Trip Planner		Create trips and calculate estimated costs
Booking			    Submit and validate booking forms
Favorites		    View and manage saved destinations
